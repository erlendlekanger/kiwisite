The virtual card

The virtual card is claimed by connecting a Solana wallet and pressing claim. The card is recorded against the wallet address that claimed it.

  • It is a visual representation only. The virtual card has no card number, expiry date, security code or balance of its own. It cannot be used at a card terminal or in a card field at any checkout. The digits shown on it are decorative.
  • Shopping with the virtual card means buying a balance for a specific shop through this site, paid from your wallet. See Shop balances.
  • A claim is tied to one wallet address. We cannot move it to another wallet, and losing access to your wallet means losing access to the card.
  • We may change, reset, suspend or remove virtual cards at any time, for any reason, including technical reasons.

  • Any delivery time we mention is an estimate. Post can be delayed, lost, damaged, held by customs or returned. We are not responsible for the postal service, couriers or customs authorities, or for any duty, tax or fee they charge.

  • When a card is on its way, we contact you at the email address you gave. We never ask for your wallet's secret recovery phrase or private key. Anyone who does is not us.

Shop balances

The shops listed on the site, and the balances you buy for them, are supplied by a third party, Bitrefill. When you buy, an invoice is created with the supplier and paid from your wallet together with our fee.

  • What you receive is a balance, code, voucher or link issued by the supplier or the shop, for the shop and country shown. It is redeemed with that shop, under that shop's own terms.
  • Each balance only works in the country it is listed for. Choosing the wrong country or shop is your responsibility.
  • Some shops have their own limits, such as one top-up per account per day. Shops can refuse, freeze or cancel a balance under their own rules, and we cannot overrule them.
  • Prices are shown before you pay. Amounts in other currencies are converted at a rate set when the invoice is created, which can differ from the figure shown a moment earlier.
  • Delivery is usually quick but is not guaranteed. A supplier can be slow, out of stock or unavailable.
  • Keep your code private. Anyone who has it can use it, and a used or shared code cannot be replaced.

Fees

  • Virtual card: free to claim. Each shop purchase carries a fee of 4% of the supplier price, with a minimum of $0.25, shown in full before you sign.
  • Physical card: 1% of every top-up to the card.
  • Network fees: every Solana transaction costs a small network fee, paid to the network and not to us. Paying with a token other than USDC includes a swap, which can carry price impact.

Fees can change at any time. The fee that applies is the one shown at the moment you approve the transaction. Fees are not refunded.

Payments and finality
