Under the hood

Positions, not tips.

On every cycle the index reads each trader's wallet on-chain, blends their holdings by your weights, and trades your basket to match. Dust, airdrops and illiquid tokens are left out automatically.

Tempo & fees

You set the pace. The fee follows.

Choose how often the index checks your traders and trades toward them. Faster means tighter tracking but more swaps, so the fee per trade goes up. Slower is cheaper and calmer.

The fee is taken only when the index actually trades, as a share of that trade. Quiet rounds cost nothing. Solana network fees and slippage come on top and are paid by the index.

Charged per swap, not per cycle

A cycle with no trades costs nothing. The fee is a percentage of each buy or sell the index executes.

Threshold keeps it calm

The index only trades when a position drifts more than 2.5% from target, so even one-minute indexes don't churn in a flat market.

Safety

Rules the index never breaks.

Hard limits in the engine, not settings you have to remember. They are the same for every index.

Liquidity floor

A token needs at least $50k of liquidity before the index will touch it. Thin tokens are skipped even if a trader holds them.

Dust is ignored

Anything under 2% of a trader's wallet is treated as noise: airdrops, leftovers, lottery tickets.

At most 12 tokens

The index keeps its largest positions and leaves the long tail, so fees and slippage stay small.

Threshold rebalancing

It only trades when a position is more than 2.5% off target. No churn, no death by a thousand swaps.

Your money, your exit

Pause any time. Withdraw any time: everything is sold to SOL and sent to the wallet you signed in with.

Encrypted keys

Each index has its own wallet. Its key is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and only ever used to swap through Jupiter.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is this copy trading?

It is the calmer cousin. Copy trading chases every entry a trader makes. An index mirrors positions: what your traders hold, blended by weight, checked as often as you choose. You get their conviction without racing their fills.

Who holds the funds?

Every index has its own Solana wallet. Its private key is generated on the server, encrypted with a master key and only decrypted for a swap or a withdrawal. You can see the wallet on Solscan at all times, and only the wallet you signed in with can withdraw.

How often does it trade?

On the interval you pick, from every minute to once a day. Each cycle it reads your traders' wallets, works out the target basket and trades only the positions that are more than 2.5% off. In a flat market most cycles do nothing.

How much does it cost?

You choose how often the index mirrors your traders, and the fee follows: 0.3% of each trade at once a day, 0.5% at every 2 hours, 1% at every 30 minutes, up to 2.5% at every minute. Faster costs more because it trades more. Solana network fees and slippage come on top, paid by the index.

What if a trader I picked rugs or goes quiet?

The liquidity floor and the dust rule keep the worst tokens out. If a trader stops trading, their weight simply sits in SOL. You can pause the index, remove it or withdraw at any time.

Minimum deposit?

0.2 SOL works. Below that, fees eat the rebalancing. 1 SOL and up is where it gets interesting.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare and Backpack in the browser, and any mobile wallet that opens the page in its in-app browser. You sign a message to log in; nothing is approved or spent by signing.

Can I change the traders later?

Not yet. Withdraw, build a new index and fund it. Editing a live index is next on the list.
