Build your index How it works
THE PICK

traders from the fomo leaderboard

Choose up to ten. Each card shows what the trader holds right now, read straight from their wallet on-chain, with their rank and 7-day PnL.

WEIGHTS

equal split, or lean in

40 / 35 / 25

Every trader's holdings count toward your basket in proportion to their weight. Equal by default, sliders if you have a favourite.

THE MIRROR

positions, not tips

On every cycle the index reads each trader's wallet, blends the holdings by your weights and trades your basket to match. What they sell, it sells. What they buy, it buys next round.

TEMPO

as fast as you like

1 min → 24 h

You choose how often it checks and trades. Faster tracks tighter and pays a higher fee per trade. Two hours is the sweet spot for most.

FEES

charged per swap, only when it trades

0.5%

At every two hours. From 0.3% once a day up to 2.5% every minute. A quiet round costs nothing. Solana fees and slippage come on top.

YOUR EXIT

withdraw any time

24/7

Pause or sell everything back to SOL and send it to the wallet you signed in with, whenever you want. Each index has its own wallet you can watch on Solscan.

Tempo & fees

You set the pace. The fee follows.

Faster mirroring means more swaps, so the fee per trade goes up. Slower is cheaper and calmer. The index only trades when a position drifts more than 2.5% from target, so even fast indexes stay quiet in a flat market.

Rules

Limits the index never breaks.

Hard limits in the engine, the same for every index. Not settings you have to remember.

LIQUIDITY FLOOR

$50k or it is skipped

A token needs at least $50k of liquidity before the index will touch it. Thin tokens are left out even if a trader holds them.

DUST

under 2% is noise

Anything under 2% of a trader's wallet is ignored: airdrops, leftovers, lottery tickets.

POSITIONS

at most twelve tokens

12

The index keeps its largest positions and leaves the long tail, so fees and slippage stay small.

THRESHOLD

trades only when it has to

2.5%

A position has to be more than 2.5% off target before the index trades it. No churn.

ROUTING

every swap through Jupiter

Best route across Solana's liquidity, with a 1.5% slippage cap. If a route is worse than that, the trade waits for the next cycle.

CUSTODY

one wallet per index, encrypted

The key is generated on the server, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, and only ever used to swap or to withdraw to the wallet you signed in with. FOMOINDEX is not made by FOMO.
