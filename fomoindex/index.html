How it works

Three choices. Then it runs itself.

No charts to read, no entries to time. You decide who to follow and how much. The index does the trading.

01

Pick traders

Choose from the fomo leaderboard. See what each trader holds right now, their 7-day PnL and rank, and add up to ten of them to your index.

02

Set weights

Equal split by default, or lean into your favourites with a slider. Each trader's holdings count toward your index in proportion to their weight.

03

Fund with SOL

Send SOL to your index's own wallet. Within minutes it buys what your traders hold. From then on it sells what they sell and buys what they buy.

Under the hood

Positions, not tips.

Every 30 minutes the index reads each trader's wallet on-chain, blends their holdings by your weights, and trades your basket to match. Dust, airdrops and illiquid tokens are left out automatically.

Safety

Rules the index never breaks.

Liquidity floor

A token needs at least $50k of liquidity before the index will touch it. Thin tokens are skipped even if a trader holds them.

Dust is ignored

Anything under 2% of a trader's wallet is treated as noise: airdrops, leftovers, lottery tickets.

At most 12 tokens

The index keeps its largest positions and leaves the long tail, so fees and slippage stay small.

Threshold rebalancing

It only trades when a position is more than 2.5% off target. No churn, no death by a thousand swaps.

Your money, your exit

Pause any time. Withdraw any time: everything is sold to SOL and sent to the wallet you signed in with.

Encrypted keys

Each index has its own wallet. Its key is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and only ever used to swap through Jupiter.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is this copy trading?

It is the calmer cousin. Copy trading chases every entry a trader makes. An index mirrors positions: what your traders hold, blended by weight, checked every 30 minutes. You get their conviction without racing their fills.

Who holds the funds?

Every index has its own Solana wallet. Its private key is generated on the server, encrypted with a master key and only decrypted for a swap or a withdrawal. You can see the wallet on Solscan at all times, and only the wallet you signed in with can withdraw.

What if a trader I picked rugs or goes quiet?

The liquidity floor and the dust rule keep the worst tokens out. If a trader stops trading, their weight simply sits in SOL. You can pause the index, remove it or withdraw at any time.

How much does it cost?

Swap fees and slippage on Solana are paid by the index, nothing else is charged during the beta. Fees, if any, will be shown here before they apply.

Minimum deposit?

0.2 SOL works. Below that, fees eat the rebalancing. 1 SOL and up is where it gets interesting.

Can I change the traders later?

Not yet. Withdraw, build a new index and fund it. Editing a live index is next on the list.
