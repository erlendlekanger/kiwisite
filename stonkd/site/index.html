02 · How it works

From one trade to a payout

  1. 01

    The coin launches paired with a stock

    You pick the pair, the recipient and a dev buy, then sign one payment. The pool is created and your coin is bought in the same transaction, and the tokens land in your wallet.

  2. 02

    Every trade leaves a fee behind

    The coin trades against the stock on its own curve. Each trade pays a creator fee in the pair token, so a coin paired with Apple earns Apple.

  3. 03

    The fees are collected and turned into SOL

    Every few minutes the fees are collected from the coin's own creator account and swapped to SOL. Each collection is listed on the coin page with its transaction.

  4. 04

    The recipient is paid in SOL, or the holders in stocks

    An X profile, a GitHub account or a wallet is paid in SOL. A coin can instead pay its holders, in SOL or in up to five stocks bought with the fees. The platform keeps a fixed share of every fee.
